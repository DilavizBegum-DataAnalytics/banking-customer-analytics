# 🏦 Banking Customer Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior, transaction patterns, revenue, and profitability for a banking dataset covering **January 2019 to December 2025**.

The objective is to transform raw banking transaction data into meaningful business insights that can help identify high-value customers, profitable transaction types, revenue trends, and potential opportunities for business growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The bank wants to understand:

- Which customers generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which transaction types are most profitable?
- How do customers behave across different channels?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Are there indicators of declining customer activity?
- What opportunities exist to improve revenue and profitability?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly and yearly transaction trends
- Identify high-revenue and high-profit customers
- Measure overall profitability
- Evaluate transaction types and channels
- Analyze customer revenue and profit patterns
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Transaction Trend

The transaction trend was analyzed across the reporting period to identify changes in customer activity.

The analysis showed relatively stable transaction activity across most months, with noticeable peaks and dips during certain years.

---

### 2. Customer Performance

Customer performance was analyzed across different age groups and customer segments.

The **18–38 age segment** contributed more revenue compared with other age groups.

---

### 3. Customer Lifetime Value

Customer Lifetime Value was analyzed to understand which customer groups and channels generate greater long-term value.

Online and mobile-app channels showed strong revenue contribution, while ATM and branch channels demonstrated steady growth.

---

### 4. Top 10 Customers

The analysis identified the highest-profit customers to understand the bank's most valuable customer relationships.

The top customers can be used as a basis for customer retention and targeted relationship strategies.

---

### 5. Revenue vs Profit

Revenue and profit trends were compared to understand whether revenue growth translated into profitability.

Revenue growth was particularly strong during **2021 and 2022**, while profit increased alongside revenue.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 💡 Insight 1 – Customer Segment

Customers between **18 and 38 years** generated a higher share of revenue compared with other age segments.

### 💡 Insight 2 – Digital Channels

Online and mobile-app channels contributed significantly to revenue, highlighting the importance of digital banking services.

### 💡 Insight 3 – Customer Value

A relatively small group of high-value customers contributed significantly to overall profitability.

### 💡 Insight 4 – Transaction Trends

Transaction activity remained relatively stable overall, with noticeable peaks and declines across different years.

### 💡 Insight 5 – Revenue & Profit

Revenue growth during 2021 and 2022 was accompanied by corresponding profit growth.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

1. **Strengthen premium customer offerings**  
   Provide targeted benefits and personalized services to high-value customers.

2. **Invest in digital banking channels**  
   Continue improving online and mobile banking experiences due to their strong revenue contribution.

3. **Focus on high-value customer retention**  
   Develop personalized offers and relationship strategies for customers generating significant profit.

4. **Target younger customer segments**  
   Since customers aged 18–38 contribute strongly to revenue, targeted digital products and campaigns could help increase engagement.

5. **Monitor transaction and profitability trends**  
   Regular KPI monitoring can help identify changes in customer activity and business performance early.
